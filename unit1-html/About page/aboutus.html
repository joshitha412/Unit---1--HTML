About Us

Prince Dr. K. Vasudevan College of Engineering and Technology (PDKVCET), located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, was established in 2009 under the Prince Educational Society. The institution is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated with Anna University, Chennai.

PDKVCET is committed to providing quality technical education with a strong emphasis on academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development. The college offers a range of undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs supported by experienced faculty, modern laboratories, smart classrooms, and a well-equipped library.

Our institution focuses on industry-oriented learning, research, and entrepreneurship to prepare students for professional success. Along with academics, students are encouraged to participate in co-curricular and extracurricular activities to develop leadership, teamwork, and ethical values.

Guided by the principles of Truth, Love, and Discipline, PDKVCET strives to nurture responsible engineers and technologists who contribute positively to society.
